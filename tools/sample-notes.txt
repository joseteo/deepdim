Night reading notes

Brightness is already at its lowest setting,
the room is dark, and the page still glows
like a lamp pointed at the eyes.

Deep Dim scales every pixel GNOME Shell draws,
so the screen goes far below the panel minimum:

  - slide left in Quick Settings for less light
  - lower the contrast so white pages stop glaring
  - add warmth to cut blue light before sleep
  - leave a second screen untouched

Super+Alt+End turns it off again at any time.
